Selected research contributions
======
* **Robot perception and autonomous manipulation:** Developed vision-based perception and state-estimation methods for robot-assisted microsurgery and laparoscopic interventions, including foundation-model-assisted instrument tracking, marker-based pose estimation, and error-state Kalman filtering. Investigated perception-driven robotic manipulation under uncertain visual observations.
* **Safe and adaptive surgical robot control:** Developed shared-control and constrained-control strategies for microsurgical and hard-tissue interventions, including adaptive admittance control, force-guided virtual fixtures, hybrid vision–force control, and optimization-based motion constraints for safe human–robot interaction.
* **Robot learning and optimization:** Investigated learning-based and model-based approaches to surgical robot autonomy, including Gaussian mixture model-based skill representation, uncertainty-aware kinematic calibration, constrained trajectory optimization, and robotic dynamics for model predictive control. Contributed to the open-source OpTaS robotics optimization library.
