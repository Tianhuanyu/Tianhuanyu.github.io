## Summary
This work presents a weakly supervised robotic micromanipulation framework that learns calibration-aware perception from reusable warm-up trajectories and combines it with admittance control. The agent supports closed-loop, depth-resolved microscope-guided manipulation without external fiducials or frame-by-frame depth annotation.
